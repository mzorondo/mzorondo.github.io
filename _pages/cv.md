Education
======
* Ph.D. in Innovation and Management, Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore, *ongoing (2023–)*
  * Literature review on the potential of a Universal Basic Income (UBI) as a driver of innovation
  * Studying natural experiments of UBI-like policies during COVID-19
  * Modeling the introduction and long-term effects of a UBI using Agent-Based Models (extending the Keynes+Schumpeter framework)
* M.Sc. in Physics, Universidad de Chile, 2021
  * Statistical-mechanical model of Thomson scattering of electromagnetic waves from a nonequilibrium plasma
  * Designed and implemented an experimental Thomson scattering setup for a Plasma Focus discharge
* B.Sc. in Physics, Universidad de Chile, 2015
  * Studied solar activity through sunspot evolution using complex networks

Research focus
======
* Universal Basic Income (UBI) as a driver of entrepreneurship, innovation, and economic growth
* Agent-based computational economics (ACE), heterogeneous-agent and Keynes+Schumpeter (K+S) macroeconomic modeling
* Entrepreneurial entry dynamics and necessity entrepreneurship under labor-market stress
* The future of work: automation, income guarantees, and market dynamism

Experience
======
* 2015–2023: Research Assistant, Research Center on the Intersection in Plasma Physics, Matter and Complexity (P2mc), Chilean Nuclear Energy Commission
  * First complete characterization of the fast dynamics of the Multipurpose Generator in Plasma Focus configuration at high total neutron output, via shadowgraphy
  * Implemented simultaneous Mach-Zehnder interferometry and shadowgraphy on the Multipurpose Generator Plasma Focus device
  * Characterized neutron emission of the Multipurpose Generator Plasma Focus device
* Mar 2021 – Sep 2021: Research Assistant, Faculty of Science, University of Chile
  * Constructed and validated a multimodal analytical rubric for assessing the quality of laboratory physics reports
* Jan 2015: Summer Intern, Optics and Plasma Physics Group, Institute of Physics, Pontifical Catholic University of Chile
* Aug 2014 – Dec 2014: Intern, Non-linear Optics Group, Faculty of Science, University of Chile
* Mar 2014 – Dec 2014: Intern, Group of Complex Systems, Faculty of Science, University of Chile
