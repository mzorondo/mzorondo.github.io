An ongoing extension of the Keynes+Schumpeter (K+S) agent-based macroeconomic model, implemented in LSD (C++), that introduces an explicit **entrepreneurial-entry mechanism**: agents can transition from employee to owner-worker based on a Bernoulli entry probability tied to the unemployment-to-vacancy ratio, capturing *necessity entrepreneurship* — the idea that high unemployment and scarce vacancies lower the opportunity cost of starting a firm.

The model is designed to explore how a Universal Basic Income changes this calculus: does it crowd out necessity-driven entry, enable more opportunity-driven entrepreneurship, or stabilize firm formation over the business cycle?
