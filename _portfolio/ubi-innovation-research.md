My Ph.D. research at Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore asks a simple question with a complicated answer: **can a Universal Basic Income drive entrepreneurship, innovation, and market dynamism, rather than just reduce poverty?**

The project has three strands:

1. **Literature review** on UBI's theoretical capacity to act as an innovation driver, including debates on universality (e.g. the Korpi–Palme paradox of redistribution) and perspectives from across the political spectrum.
2. **Natural experiments**, studying policies functionally similar to a UBI introduced during COVID-19, alongside major pilot studies (GiveDirectly Kenya, the Alaska Permanent Fund, Finland's basic income trial, OpenResearch's US study, Namibia, and India).
3. **Agent-based modeling**, using and extending the Keynes+Schumpeter macroeconomic framework to simulate the long-run effects of introducing a UBI on labor markets, firm entry, and growth.
